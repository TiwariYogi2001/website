Featured case study

From 500 Steam pages to a signed studio partnership

  1. 500+ Steam titles scraped & analysed
  2. Tiered studios scored by fit
  3. 100+ studios & publishers contacted
  4. 30%+ replied — 3–5× industry
  5. 1 partnership signed

Brothers Interactive sells outsourced art and development to game studios. Cold outreach in this industry usually gets a 5–10% reply rate, because most messages are generic and land with studios that have no current need. The question: which studios should we talk to, and what should we say so they actually reply?

I wrote a 1,310-line Python scraper, turned the output into a lead-scoring model, and built outreach around what each studio was actually shipping.
