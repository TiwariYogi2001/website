Hello,
I'm TIWARI!

Business Analyst | Data Analytics Enthusiast | B2B Insights Strategist

My professional journey has always been guided by curiosity and a desire to create meaningful solutions. I started as a Unity Developer at Mobzway Technologies, where writing gameplay logic and shaping interactive experiences taught me how to think clearly, design efficiently, and solve problems creatively. That foundation led me into the world of AR/VR at AIC MUJ Innovation Foundation, where I worked on immersive prototypes, supported startup ideas, and collaborated closely with clients to turn concepts into working experiences. It was here that I discovered how powerful technology can be when aligned with real business needs. Over time, I realized that behind every effective solution, there is data quietly shaping the direction—and that realization pulled me toward analytics. Today, as a Business Analyst at NextLeap, I bring together my development background and analytical mindset to convert raw data into insights that support strategic decision-making. I build dashboards, uncover trends, and streamline reporting while also contributing to project acquisition by understanding client challenges and preparing data-driven proposals. Each phase of my journey has strengthened my ability to approach problems with creativity, logic, and a strong focus on delivering real business value.
