The question

Callbreak is a trick-taking card game popular in South Asia. Make it play smoothly online, in real time, on mobile.

The data

Part of Mobzway's 2D casino game portfolio.

Approach

  1. 01

    Rules

    Turn management, bidding logic, scoring rules and fair card distribution.

  2. 02

    Multiplayer

    Online matchmaking and real-time communication between players over Socket.io.

  3. 03

    Bots

    Bot players so tables fill and games continue when someone drops.

  4. 04

    Polish

    Clean UI, animations, and performance tuning of game loops for mobile.

What I found
