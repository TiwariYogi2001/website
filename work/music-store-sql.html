-- Where should we hold a promotional festival?
-- The city whose customers spend the most.
SELECT billing_city,
       SUM(total) AS invoice_total
FROM invoice
GROUP BY billing_city
ORDER BY invoice_total DESC
LIMIT 1;

The question

A digital music store wants to reward its best customers and plan a promotional music festival in the right city.

The data

Employees, customers, invoices and invoice lines, tracks, albums, artists, genres, media types and playlists.

Approach

  1. 01

    Basics

    Senior-most employee, invoices by country, top invoice totals.

  2. 02

    Customers

    The best city by invoice total, and the single highest-spending customer.

  3. 03

    Genres

    Rock listeners, top artists by track count, and the most popular genre in each country.

What I found

  • Prague is the festival city: $273 in invoices, well ahead of Mountain View ($169) and London ($166).
  • The USA has the most invoices (131), followed by Canada (76) and Brazil (61).
  • The single best customer spent $144.54 — the top three together account for 8% of all revenue.
  • Rock dominates with 2,635 tracks sold, more than four times Metal in second place.
