The question

Where does the money go each month, and when does spending overtake income? A personal budget is a small dataset with the same questions a CFO asks.

The data

A year of debit and credit transactions, categorised into 8 main categories and 19 sub-categories.

Approach

  1. 01

    Categorise

    Mapped every transaction into a two-level category tree.

  2. 02

    Measure

    DAX measures for debit, credit and net saving by month.

  3. 03

    Filter

    Slicers for category, sub-category and quarter so any slice can be isolated, e.g. “Q2 discretionary only”.

What I found

  • Spending is less than half of income, leaving ₹0.85M net saving.
  • Living expenses (₹393.6K) and discretionary spend (₹168.7K) are the biggest categories; rent, groceries and clothes top the sub-categories.
  • April has the highest spending (₹83K); January the highest income (₹336K).
  • November goes negative — expenses exceed income by ₹17.3K.
