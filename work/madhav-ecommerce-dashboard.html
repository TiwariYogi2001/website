The question

An online retailer has healthy revenue but thin profit. Where is the money made, where is it lost, and who are the best customers?

The data

Order and order-detail tables for a fictional e-commerce store: amount, profit, quantity, category, sub-category, customer, state, payment mode and date.

Approach

  1. 01

    Model

    Related the order and detail tables and built calculated measures for profit and average order value.

  2. 02

    Visualise

    Bar, donut, clustered bar, line and area charts, with slicers to drill into any state or category.

  3. 03

    Read

    Compared monthly profit, category mix, payment preferences and top customers.

What I found

  • Maharashtra leads sales at ₹100K+, followed by Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.
  • Clothing is 62.6% of units, electronics 20.6%, furniture 16.8%.
  • Cash on delivery still leads payments at 43.7%, ahead of UPI at 20.6%.
  • December is the best month, but May, June, July and October run at a loss.
  • Printers (₹8.6K), bookcases (₹6.5K) and sarees (₹4.1K) are the most profitable sub-categories.

What I'd recommend

  • Review pricing and discounting in the four loss-making months.
  • Nudge COD buyers toward UPI to cut return and collection costs.
  • Push high-margin sub-categories alongside high-volume clothing.
